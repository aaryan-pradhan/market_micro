Roadmap & sources

Order: mechanics → cost of trading → why the spread exists (inventory, then information) → what prices reveal → the book as a process → trading against it (execution) → quoting into it (market making) → the modern venue landscape.

  1. Harris, Trading and Exchanges: parts 1–2 (vocabulary, no maths).
  2. O'Hara, Market Microstructure Theory: parts 3–4.
  3. Hasbrouck, Empirical Market Microstructure: part 5.
  4. Bouchaud, Bonart, Donier, Gould, Trades, Quotes and Prices: parts 6–7.
  5. Cartea, Jaimungal, Penalva, Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading: parts 7–8.
